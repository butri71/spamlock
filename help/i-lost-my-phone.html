I lost my phone: what you can do now

Find or lock your phone from another device, then call your mobile provider to block the SIM. Change your passwords, starting with your email.

Steps you can take if you lost your phone

You choose which ones to do. They’re in the order that usually helps most.

  1. 1

    Find, lock or erase it

    Open Find My (iPhone) or Find Hub (Android) below, from another phone or a computer. You’ll see where the phone is and can lock it with a message on screen. Erasing can’t be undone, so it’s usually the last thing to try.

    Find My (iPhone)Find Hub (Android)
  2. 2

    Block the SIM

    Call your mobile provider and ask them to block it, so no one can use your number for calls, texts or sign-in codes.

  3. 3

    Change your passwords

    Start with your email, because it can reset your other passwords. Then your bank and social accounts.

  4. 4

    Report it to the police

    Insurers often ask for the report.

  5. 5

    Watch for strange messages

    And for sign-in emails you don’t expect.
