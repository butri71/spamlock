My email was hacked: what you can do now

Get back in with your email provider’s recovery page, set a new password and turn on two-step verification. Then tell your contacts.

Steps you can take if your email was hacked

You choose which ones to do. They’re in the order that usually helps most.

  1. 1

    Get back into your account

    Pick your email provider below and follow its recovery steps. It checks that it’s really you, then lets you set a new password.

    GoogleMicrosoftAppleYahoo
  2. 2

    Set a new password

    One you don’t use anywhere else. If you used the old password on other accounts, you can change it there too.

  3. 3

    Turn on two-step verification

    Find it in your account’s security settings. You’ll need a code or passkey as well as your password, so a stolen password alone isn’t enough to get in.

  4. 4

    Check your recovery details

    In your account settings, look at the recovery phone, recovery email and any forwarding rules. Remove anything you don’t recognise, so the other person can’t get back in or get copies of your emails.

  5. 5

    Tell your contacts

    So they don’t open strange emails sent from your address.
