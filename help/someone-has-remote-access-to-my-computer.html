Security help/What to do if

Someone has remote access to my computer: what you can do now

Disconnect from the internet and tell your bank if they could have seen your details. Then remove the app they asked you to install, turn off remote control in your settings, run a scan, and change your passwords.

Steps you can take if someone has remote access to your computer

You choose which ones to do. They’re in the order that usually helps most.

  1. 1

    Disconnect

    If you can still see the remote session, use its disconnect button. Then turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable.

  2. 2

    Tell your bank

    Straight away if they could have seen your bank details, even if you didn’t pay. If you paid, ask your bank or card provider about getting the money back.

  3. 3

    Not confident? Get help

    From the maker’s support, a computer shop you trust, or someone you know who’s good with computers. Never from someone who contacts you first offering to fix it.

  4. 4

    Remove the app they asked you to install

    And any other app you don’t remember installing. Look at your recently installed programs.

  5. 5

    Turn off remote control in your settings

    Windows: Settings → System → Remote Desktop, and turn it off (only Windows Pro has it). Mac: System Settings → General → Sharing, and turn off Screen Sharing and Remote Management.

    Microsoft: Remote DesktopApple: screen sharing
  6. 6

    Update and run a full scan

    Windows: install the latest updates, then run a full scan with Windows Security. Mac: update macOS and restart. Apple says the Mac removes known malware when it restarts.

  7. 7

    Change your passwords

    Any password they could have seen, starting with your bank and email. Then turn on two-step verification.

  8. 8

    Still seeing strange things? Reset it

    You can reset the computer, or ask your helper to do it. On a work computer, tell your IT team.

  9. 9

    Report it

    Even if they didn’t say they were from Microsoft. Outside the UK and US, you can report it to your local police.

    UK: Report FraudUS: ReportFraud.ftc.govMicrosoft: report a scam
