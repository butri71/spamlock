Someone is threatening or harassing me online: what you can do now

Stop replying, and don’t pay if someone is blackmailing you. Take screenshots first, then block and report them. It’s not your fault, and free help is available.

Steps you can take if someone is threatening or harassing you online

You choose which ones to do. They’re in the order that usually helps most.

  1. 1

    Stop replying

    Replying keeps the pressure on. Stopping contact shows them you won’t give in.

  2. 2

    Blackmailed over private photos? Don’t pay

    Paying usually leads to more demands. If you already paid, it’s not too late to stop all contact and block them.

  3. 3

    Save the evidence first

    Take screenshots of the messages and posts, and note the username, the app or website, and the date. Do this before you block.

  4. 4

    Block and report

    Block the account, and report it to the app or website. If a new account contacts you, do the same again.

  5. 5

    Get private photos taken down

    Free tools help stop them being shared on many big platforms, without anyone seeing your photos.

    StopNCII.org (18 and over)Take It Down (under 18)
  6. 6

    Your details posted online?

    Report the post to the website or app, and make your social accounts private.

  7. 7

    Report it to the police

    Give them your screenshots and notes.

  8. 8

    Talk to someone

    In the UK, adults can contact the Revenge Porn Helpline for free help with private photos.
