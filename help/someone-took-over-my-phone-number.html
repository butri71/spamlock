My phone lost signal and someone has my number: what you can do now

Call your mobile provider from another phone and ask for your number back. Then change your email password and call your bank.

Steps you can take if someone took over your phone number

You choose which ones to do. They’re in the order that usually helps most.

  1. 1

    Call your mobile provider

    From another phone. Ask them to give your number back.

  2. 2

    Change your email password

    Thieves use your number to get sign-in codes.

  3. 3

    Call your bank

    On the number on your card, not one from a message. You can ask them to check for payments you didn’t make.

  4. 4

    Get codes from an app, not by text

    In each account’s security settings, choose an authenticator app instead of text messages. Then sign-in codes don’t go to your number.

  5. 5

    Ask for a lock on your number

    Ask your provider for a number lock or PIN, if they offer it. Then no one can move your number again without it.

  6. 6

    Report it

    It helps stop others being caught. Outside the US and UK, you can report it to your local police.

    US: ReportFraud.ftc.govUK: Report Fraud
