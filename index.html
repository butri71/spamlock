Questions people ask

What is Spamlock?

Spamlock checks your emails, passwords, links and this phone for threats, and shows you simple steps to fix them.

Where does the breach information come from?

From Have I Been Pwned, a public list of data breaches. Two-step sign-in options come from 2fa.directory, and link checks also use Google’s list of dangerous sites.

What does “No breach found” mean?

Your email isn’t in any breach Have I Been Pwned knows about. New breaches are added often, so you can check again from time to time.

Can Spamlock see my passwords?

No. When you check a password, it never leaves your phone. Only a short scrambled code is used to look it up.

Can Spamlock prevent my data from being exposed?

No. Spamlock can’t stop a company from being hacked, and it can’t change your accounts or settings for you. It finds what has leaked or needs attention, and guides you with simple steps you take yourself.

How do alerts for new breaches work?

Spamlock looks for new breaches in Have I Been Pwned’s list every day. When a new breach appears, it checks the emails you saved and tells you if one of them is in it.
