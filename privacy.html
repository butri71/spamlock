Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Spamlock has no accounts and no ads, and we don't sell your data. Spamlock is operated by the Spamlock development team. Questions: spamlock.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Saved emails (encrypted), your phone check-up, your ticks and your settings stay on your phone. Passwords do too: a password check sends only a 5-character fragment of a scrambled version, which can't reveal the password.

We use this data to run, protect and improve the app. Our website also uses Google Analytics, but only if you accept cookies. You can change your choice at any time in Cookie settings.

Services we use

Have I Been Pwned, Google (Firebase, Analytics, Web Risk), RevenueCat, Apple and Google app stores, Expo and 2fa.directory. Like any online service, they can see your IP address when the app connects. They may process data outside your country, including in the USA and Australia. We choose services that protect data to at least the standard in this policy.

Your choices

  • Saved emails: delete them in More → Delete saved emails, before you uninstall.
  • Notifications: turn them off in More or in your phone's settings.
  • Plus: cancel in your App Store or Google Play account.
  • Your data: to delete your data, or ask for a copy or correction, write to spamlock.app@gmail.com. With no accounts, we can't usually link records to a person, so we may need your help to find them.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Spamlock isn't for children under 13. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes

We'll update this page and its date when things change. Older versions of Spamlock send email checks directly to Have I Been Pwned.
